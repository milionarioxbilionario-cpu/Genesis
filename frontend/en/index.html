Everyday problems, solved

In a business, losses rarely come from a sale. They come from cash missing from the till, products spoiling on the shelf and the credit nobody collected.

The problem

Cash goes missing from the till

With Genesis

To close the shift, the cashier has to count the cash in the drawer and type how much they counted, without knowing how much Genesis expects. If they type less than they sold in cash, Genesis refuses it; on the third time, their profile is locked until the owner unlocks it.

See more details
  • Only cash counts (notes and coins). What was paid by M-Pesa, e-Mola or card is not part of the count: Genesis already knows those amounts.
  • Example: today the cashier sold 17,000 MT in cash. Genesis knows it, because every sale was recorded. When closing the shift, they must type 17,000 MT or more (there may be tips).
  • If they type 16,000 MT, Genesis refuses and tells them they have 2 attempts left, without saying how much it should be. If they type less again (16,900 MT, for example), 1 is left. On the third, their profile is locked and only the owner can unlock it.
  • Each wrong attempt is recorded with the amount they typed and what Genesis expected, and the owner sees it as soon as they open Genesis.
  • If they closed the shift saying they counted 17,000 MT and at the end of the day hand you 16,000 MT, the conversation is simple: “You closed the shift saying you counted 17,000 MT. Where is the rest?”

The problem

Products that go past their expiry date

With Genesis

Genesis warns you before a product expires and puts on sale first what expires first. Whatever spoils is noted as a loss: you know how much you lost and why.

The problem

Forgotten customer credit

With Genesis

Every credit ("txeneka") keeps the name, contact, amount and agreed day. Genesis reminds you on the day and when it is overdue, and every payment is noted.

The problem

Not knowing your real profit

With Genesis

At the end of the month, Genesis takes salaries, rent, expenses and what you paid suppliers away from what came in. You know how much is really left for you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to sell?

No. The cashier just needs to sign in once with internet on that device. After that, they sell even without a network, and the sales go through on their own when the internet is back.

What if someone picks up my phone?

The panel asks for your PIN (or Face ID / fingerprint) every time you open it and after 5 minutes idle. And one tap on the eye hides every amount from the screen.

What equipment do I need?

A computer, tablet or phone with Chrome, Edge or Safari. A barcode scanner and a receipt printer are optional: without a printer, the receipt is a PDF.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The panel and the terminal work on a phone and can sit on the home screen, like an app.

Can cashiers see the business profit?

No. Cashiers only sell, print the receipt and note what spoiled or what customers asked for. Costs, profits and reports are for the owner only.

How much does it cost?

8,000 MT per month, everything included. New customers do not pay the first month.

How do I pay and how do I cancel?

For now, we arrange payment and cancellation with you on WhatsApp.

Can I use Genesis in English?

Yes. Each person chooses Portuguese or English, and the choice is kept.
