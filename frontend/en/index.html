Everyday problems, solved

What costs a small shop the most is rarely a sale. It is the missing cash, the stock that expires and the credit nobody collected.

Cash goes missing

At the end of the shift the cashier counts the cash without seeing how much there should be. Genesis compares and the owner sees the difference. Three counts below lock the profile until the owner decides.

Stock disappears or expires

Stock by batch, with expiry dates. What expires first always leaves first, the alert arrives before it expires and losses are recorded with the reason.

Customer credit is forgotten

Every credit ("cheneca") with name, phone, amount and due date. The alert shows the ones due today, tomorrow or already overdue.

You do not know your real profit

The monthly report subtracts salaries, rent, expenses and supplier deliveries. You see the net profit, not just what came into the till.

Everything the shop needs, in one system

A fast sales terminal

Accent-insensitive search, barcode scanner, phone camera and keyboard shortcuts. Each cashier signs in with their own 4-digit PIN.

Sells without internet

Sales are kept on the device and are sent automatically when the network is back. Each sale has its own number, so it is never counted twice.

Cash, M-Pesa, e-Mola and Card

Each payment method appears separately in the reports and in the shift close.

Owner PIN for sensitive actions

Cancelling a sale, a discount above the limit or reopening the shift needs the owner's PIN. Wrong attempts are recorded.

Receipts with a verification QR

Receipts as PDF or printed. The customer scans the QR and confirms the receipt is genuine.

Daily, weekly and monthly reports

Best-selling and most profitable products, comparison with the previous period, sales lost to missing products and a PDF to share.

Suggested shopping list

Genesis suggests what to buy based on sales. The list goes to the supplier on WhatsApp in one tap.

Alerts in one bell

Low stock, expiry dates, customer credit, shift-close differences and the monthly goal reached.

Team and goals

Cashiers, workers and salaries. A monthly sales goal with day-by-day progress.

Portuguese and English

Each person chooses their language: panel, terminal, receipts and PDFs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to sell?

No. Once the terminal has opened with a connection, it keeps selling offline and sends the sales when the connection is back. To see the reports in the owner panel you need internet.

What equipment do I need?

A computer, tablet or phone with an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari). A barcode scanner and a receipt printer are optional: without a printer, the receipt is a PDF.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The owner panel and the terminal work on a phone and can be installed on the home screen, like an app.

Can cashiers see the shop's profit?

No. Cashiers only see the terminal: they sell, print the receipt and record breakages and customer requests. Costs, margins and reports are for the owner only.

How much does it cost?

3,000 MT per month, everything included. New customers do not pay the first month.

How do I pay and how do I cancel?

For now, payment and cancellation are arranged with us on WhatsApp.

Can I use Genesis in English?

Yes. Each person chooses Portuguese or English, and the choice is saved in the account.
